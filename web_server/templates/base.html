Complete Setup Guide

How QwikPrint Works (Process Tree)

QwikPrint connects your Xerox counter directly with customers' smartphones using QR codes and an automated Windows desktop print spooler app.

1

1. Create Shopkeeper Account

Sign up instantly using Google. Your shop receives a unique Shop ID, 7-Day Free Trial, and API License Key.

2

2. Download Windows Spooler App & Connect Printer

Download the Windows desktop print agent on your counter PC. Paste your API License Key and select your connected USB/Network printer.

Download Spooler App (.exe)
3

3. Print Counter QR Standee

Print your official QwikPrint Counter Poster PDF and place it on your counter so customers can scan and upload files.

4

4. Customer Scans & Uploads

Customer scans QR code on smartphone. They pick document/photos, paper size, color mode, 2-in-1 Aadhaar layout, and see exact total price.

5

5. Instant Counter Printing

Job appears on Live Print Queue. Customer pays Cash or Online, and connected Windows Printer automatically prints pages!
